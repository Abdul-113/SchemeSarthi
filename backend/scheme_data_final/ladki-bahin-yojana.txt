Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana
Maharashtra
Women & Child Development Department, Government of Maharashtra
Women, Financial Assistance, DBT, Monthly Allowance

Details
The "Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana" (Chief Minister's My Dear Sister Scheme) is a flagship welfare scheme launched by the Government of Maharashtra to provide direct monthly financial assistance to eligible women in the state. The scheme aims to empower women financially, enhance their health and nutrition, and strengthen their role in family decision-making. The financial assistance is transferred directly to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). The scheme covers married women, widowed women, divorced women, abandoned women, destitute women, and unmarried women in the family. This is a state-funded scheme exclusively for women residents of Maharashtra.

Benefits
The eligible beneficiary women receive Rs.1,500 per month (Rs.18,000 per year) directly into their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.
The amount is transferred via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
The benefit is provided to improve the financial independence, health, and nutritional status of women.

Eligibility
The applicant must be a woman (female).
The applicant must be a resident/domicile of the state of Maharashtra.
The applicant's age must be between 21 years and 65 years.
The combined annual family income of the applicant must not exceed Rs.2,50,000 (Two Lakh Fifty Thousand Rupees).
The applicant can be married, widowed, divorced, abandoned, destitute, or an unmarried woman in the family.

Exclusion Criteria (Not Eligible):
The applicant is NOT eligible if any of the following conditions apply:
1. The combined annual family income exceeds Rs.2,50,000.
2. Any member of the family is an income tax payer.
3. Any family member is a regular/permanent employee in a government department, undertaking, or board, or is receiving a pension after retirement from such service. Exception: Outsourced workers, contract workers, and voluntary workers are not excluded.
4. Any family member is a present or former Member of Parliament (MP) or Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA).
5. Any family member owns a four-wheeler vehicle (excluding tractors used for agriculture).

Application Process
Online
Step 1: Visit the official portal at https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in
Step 2: Register with your Aadhaar number and mobile number.
Step 3: Fill in the application form with personal details, family income, bank account details, and upload required documents.
Step 4: Submit the application online.
Step 5: The application will be verified by the local authority (Gram Sevak / Talathi for rural areas, or Ward Officer for urban areas).
Step 6: Upon successful verification, the beneficiary will start receiving Rs.1,500 per month in their bank account.

Offline
Applications may also be submitted through local Anganwadi centers, Zilla Parishad offices, or Gram Panchayat offices. Forms are available at these locations.

Documents Required
1. Aadhaar Card of the applicant (must be linked with bank account).
2. Domicile Certificate / Residential Proof of Maharashtra.
3. Income Certificate showing family annual income does not exceed Rs.2,50,000.
4. Bank Account Passbook / Details (Aadhaar-linked account).
5. Recent Passport-Sized Photograph of the applicant.
6. Age Proof (Birth Certificate, School Leaving Certificate, or Aadhaar Card).
7. Ration Card (if available).
8. Marriage Certificate (if applicable for married applicants).
9. Death Certificate of husband (if widow).
10. Divorce decree / Court order (if divorced).
11. Self-declaration of abandonment / destitution (if applicable).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana?
It is a Maharashtra state government scheme that provides Rs.1,500 per month to eligible women to improve their financial independence and well-being.

Who is eligible for this scheme?
Women who are residents of Maharashtra, aged between 21 and 65 years, with a combined family annual income not exceeding Rs.2,50,000.

What is the benefit amount?
Rs.1,500 per month (Rs.18,000 per year) transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT.

Can unmarried women apply?
Yes, unmarried women in the family are also eligible to apply.

Is this scheme only for married women?
No. Married, widowed, divorced, abandoned, destitute, and unmarried women are all eligible.

What is the income limit?
The combined annual family income must not exceed Rs.2,50,000.

What is the age limit?
The applicant must be between 21 years and 65 years of age.

Is this scheme available outside Maharashtra?
No, this scheme is exclusively for women residents of Maharashtra.

Do I need an Aadhaar-linked bank account?
Yes, the benefit is transferred via DBT and requires an Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Are government employees eligible?
No. If any family member is a regular/permanent government employee or pensioner, the applicant is not eligible. However, outsourced, contract, and voluntary workers are not excluded.

What if my family member owns a car?
If any family member owns a four-wheeler (excluding tractors), the applicant is not eligible.
